# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who previously used Arc or Moves and now want to bring their historical timeline data into Path. They may not be technical, may be handling a large personal location archive, and need calm guidance through a task that feels sensitive and irreversible.

## Product Purpose

Path Import converts Arc and Moves backups into a Path import database entirely on the user's device. Success means the user can choose a backup, understand whether the data was recognized, follow conversion progress without anxiety, and finish with a database ready for Path import.

## Brand Personality

Helpful, calm, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a careful data recovery assistant: plain-spoken, reassuring, and focused on the next useful action.

## Anti-references

Avoid SaaS marketing aesthetics, oversized hero claims, decorative dashboard chrome, generic startup gradients, and dense developer-tool presentation as the default. The app should not feel like a landing page or an admin panel.

## Design Principles

Lead with the user's next step.
Make privacy and local processing visible without turning them into marketing.
Separate scan facts, conversion state, and final results so the user never has to infer what is happening.
Show detail after the primary action is clear.
Treat warnings and review notes as explainable import outcomes, not alarming failures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard operation, readable contrast, clear focus states, text that does not rely on color alone, and restrained motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.
